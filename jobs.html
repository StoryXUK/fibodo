Why work at fibodo?

fibodo is building the operating system behind experience-led services. We're a small, motivated team shipping quickly, learning fast, and building with AI and modern tooling. If you like momentum, clear ownership, and solving real problems, you'll feel at home here.

What you'll do here

  • Build products that real businesses rely on
  • Solve operational problems with smart tech (including AI)
  • Work closely with customers and move fast from insight to release
  • Take ownership — not just tickets

What you'll get

  • A motivated team with high standards
  • Space to think, build, test, and improve
  • Modern stack, modern approach, minimal bureaucracy
  • A lot of responsibility (with support)
  • Work that's useful, not performative

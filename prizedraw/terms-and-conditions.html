Gym Operator Conference Prize Draw

Independent Gyms – Gym Operator Conference 2026
Delegate Prize Competition – Terms & Conditions

1. Promoter

The promoter of this competition is Independent Gyms (referred to as “the Promoter”).

2. Eligibility

The competition is open to registered delegates attending the Independent Gyms Gym Operator Conference 2026, taking place on 18–19 November 2026 at Cranmore Park, Solihull.

Entrants must be aged 18 or over.

Only delegates who physically attend the conference are eligible to participate and win.

Exhibitors, event staff and individuals involved in organising or administering the competition are not eligible to enter.

The Promoter reserves the right to verify eligibility and disqualify any entrant who does not meet these conditions.

3. How to Enter

Delegates can enter by scanning the unique QR codes displayed by exhibiting brands throughout the conference exhibition.

Each valid exhibitor QR code scan counts as one entry into the competition.

Delegates are encouraged to visit and engage with as many exhibiting brands as possible to maximise their entries.

Each exhibitor QR code can be scanned once per delegate for one valid entry.

Entries must be recorded through the official competition system during the conference.

Only valid, verified scans recorded by the competition system will count towards the final entry total.

Automated, fraudulent or otherwise manipulated entries will be disqualified.

There is no additional purchase necessary to participate.

4. Prizes

There are three prizes available:

First Prize: A Hyperice product bundle with an approximate retail value of £1,300.

Second Prize: A £500 voucher to spend with any exhibiting brand participating in the Gym Operator Conference 2026.

Third Prize: Six months of Independent Gyms membership, valued at £180 + VAT.

Prizes are as described and:

Have no cash or monetary alternative.

Cannot be exchanged, transferred or redeemed for cash.

Cannot be substituted for other products, services or benefits unless agreed by the Promoter.

Are subject to any reasonable fulfilment arrangements agreed between the Promoter and the relevant prize provider.

5. £500 Exhibitor Voucher

The £500 voucher must be redeemed with an exhibiting brand participating in the Gym Operator Conference 2026.

The voucher must be used in full or any remaining balance redeemed by 31 December 2026.

Any unused balance after this date will be forfeited.

The voucher cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to another person.

Any purchase exceeding the £500 voucher value must be paid for by the winner directly to the relevant exhibitor.

The winner is responsible for agreeing the purchase and redemption arrangements directly with their chosen exhibitor.

6. Winner Selection

Winners will be determined by the total number of valid exhibitor QR code scans recorded against each eligible delegate during the competition period.

The three delegates with the highest number of valid entries will be awarded the three prizes, in descending order of entries.

The delegate with the highest number of entries will receive first prize, the second-highest will receive second prize and the third-highest will receive third prize.

In the event of a tie, the Promoter will use the time at which the tied entrant reached their final entry total as the deciding factor. The entrant who reached that total first will be ranked higher.

The Promoter's decision regarding the validity of entries and final competition results will be final.

7. Winner Notification

Winners will be contacted using the contact details provided when registering for the conference.

Winners may be announced through Independent Gyms' communication channels, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

Winners must respond within 7 days of being contacted to confirm acceptance of their prize.

If a winner cannot be contacted, does not respond within the specified period or is found to be ineligible, the Promoter reserves the right to award the prize to the next eligible delegate in the rankings.

8. Data Protection and GDPR

Independent Gyms will process personal data collected through the competition for the purposes of administering entries, calculating results, verifying eligibility, contacting winners and fulfilling prizes.

By scanning an exhibitor's unique QR code, delegates acknowledge and agree that their relevant contact information will be shared with that specific exhibitor.

Each exhibitor will receive the information associated with the delegate scan for its own business purposes, in accordance with its applicable privacy policy.

Scanning an exhibitor's QR code does not automatically subscribe a delegate to that exhibitor's marketing communications. Any marketing communications must be subject to the exhibitor's own applicable privacy and consent requirements.

Delegates' personal data will not be shared with other exhibitors simply by entering the competition. Data will only be shared with exhibitors whose QR codes the delegate has chosen to scan, together with any necessary service providers involved in administering the competition.

Independent Gyms will handle personal information in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, including the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018.

Delegates have rights in relation to their personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion where applicable. Further information on how Independent Gyms handles personal data can be found in its Privacy Policy: https://independentgyms.co.uk/privacy-policy/

Privacy

Privacy, respected by default.

fibodo is designed to handle personal and operational data responsibly — with privacy embedded into how the platform works. We believe trust starts with transparency.

1. Privacy-First Design

Our systems collect only data that is necessary; use data only for legitimate, defined purposes; and avoid unnecessary duplication or exposure. Privacy considerations are built into product decisions, not added later.

2. Data Ownership & Control

Customers retain ownership of their data. fibodo processes data only to provide and support the platform. We do not sell personal data. We do not use customer data for advertising.

3. User Rights

fibodo supports data subject rights in line with GDPR and applicable regulations, including right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability. Requests are handled through defined internal processes.

4. Data Retention

Data retention periods are defined and documented. Data is retained only as long as necessary. Secure deletion processes are in place.

5. Transparency

Our Privacy Policy clearly explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used and how it is protected.

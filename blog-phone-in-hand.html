Phone in Hand, Business in Motion

Running a business no longer means being tied to a desk. With the right tools in your hand, you can manage your bookings, organise your time and stay connected with your clients wherever the day takes you.

For coaches, trainers, instructors and other independent professionals, business happens everywhere. You might be moving between sessions, travelling to a venue, working outdoors or meeting clients across several locations. Your business tools need to move with you.

Your business in your pocket

A mobile business platform gives you immediate access to the information you need. Instead of waiting until you are back at your laptop, you can respond to changes and keep everything moving from your phone.

With fibodo, you can:

  • View and manage your upcoming bookings.
  • Check your live schedule and availability.
  • Manage clients and their booking history.
  • Update sessions, services and activities.
  • Receive booking and payment information.
  • Communicate important updates to customers.

Make every spare moment useful

A few minutes between appointments can be enough to confirm a booking, check tomorrow’s timetable or make a quick change to your availability. Small tasks no longer need to accumulate into hours of administration at the end of the day.

That means less time catching up and more time focused on your clients, your services and the work that earns your income.

Stay in control when plans change

Changes are part of running an active business. A client may cancel, a session might need to move or new availability may open unexpectedly.

Having your business tools on your phone means you can act immediately. Your schedule remains accurate, your customers remain informed and newly available time can be offered for somebody else to book.

Give clients greater convenience

Mobile access is not only valuable for you. Your customers also expect to find availability, book and pay using the device already in their hand.

A clear mobile booking journey removes unnecessary friction and allows people to book when their interest is highest—even when you are busy delivering another session.

  • Customers can view live availability.
  • Bookings can be made at any time.
  • Payments can be taken at the point of booking.
  • Confirmations and reminders are sent automatically.
  • Customers can manage their plans in one place.

Professional wherever you work

A professional business does not need a permanent reception desk. What matters is that customers receive a consistent, reliable experience whenever they interact with you.

By connecting your bookings, payments, schedule and customer information, fibodo helps you deliver that experience wherever your work takes place.

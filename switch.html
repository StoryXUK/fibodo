What does the Switch Guarantee include?+

Migration, setup and onboarding are free. There is no minimum term, and you can leave with 30 days’ notice. Your chosen subscription and payment processing fees still apply.

Can I move while I have an existing contract?+

Yes — we can plan your move around its end date. Any charges owed to your existing supplier remain yours; this offer does not include a contract buyout.

What happens to customer data and payments?+

We review your records and payment arrangements before scheduling the handover, agree what can transfer and check the imported information with you.

Will my customers need a new app?+

Customers can use your fibodo app to find sessions, book and manage their activity. We help you explain the change clearly before launch.

How long will the switch take?+

We agree the timetable after reviewing your current setup. You choose the launch date, with time to prepare your team.

Can I keep my website?+

Yes. Bring fibodo booking into your existing website, or use a free fibodo website. We will help you choose the best route for your business.

What support will my team receive?+

A dedicated onboarding contact guides the move and training. Our UK support team remains available after launch.

Can you check our integrations first?+

Yes. Tell us about your payment provider, access hardware and other essential tools so we can confirm compatibility before you commit.

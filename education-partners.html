CORE by fibodo

Not another app. The operating system for a learner's business.

Most new professionals end up stitching together a website, a calendar, payments, messages, client records and admin tools.

CORE brings the essentials into one connected place, so learners can focus less on figuring out the business side and more on doing the work they trained to do.

  • Look credible — Create a professional, bookable presence from day one.
  • Sell confidently — Manage sessions, services, appointments and customers in one place.
  • Keep moving — Build the habits, visibility and customer relationships that turn early momentum into a career.

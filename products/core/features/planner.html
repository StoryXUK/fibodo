THE OVERVIEW

Built to make business simpler.

Your time is split between delivery, team management, and keeping an eye on what’s coming next. The fibodo Planner pulls everything into one live diary, so you instantly see what’s on, where, and with who. As bookings come in, reschedule, or cancel, your planner updates in real time. No manual edits, no scattered calendars — just one source of truth for your whole operation.

What you can do

Live Diary Views

Switch between day, week, and month layouts to see exactly what’s happening, from a single coach’s day to your whole organisation’s schedule.

Team & Resource Overview

View multiple coaches, rooms, or locations side by side, so you can balance workloads and make better use of your space.

Drag-and-Drop Control

Move bookings between times, coaches, or locations in seconds — with all linked data and notifications updated automatically.

Capacity & Clash Management

See at a glance which sessions are full, under-used, or in conflict, and adjust your schedule before it becomes a problem.

Planner Connected to Everything

Because the Planner sits at the heart of CORE, every change you make is reflected across bookings, payments, customers, and reporting — automatically.

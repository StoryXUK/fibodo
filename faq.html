Quick answers about fibodo.

What is fibodo?

fibodo is a platform for businesses that sell time, space, services and experiences. It handles bookings, payments, customers, staff, content and growth in one place.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. CORE Free is £0 and lets you start taking bookings straight away. Paid plans add more features as you grow.

Which product is right for me?

CORE suits independent professionals and small operators. GALAXY is for growing studios and clubs. PULSAR and ENTERPRISE are coming in 2027.

Can I move my existing clients across?

Yes. Our team can help you import clients and services so you can switch without starting again.

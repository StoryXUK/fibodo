Common questions

Who is fibodo built for?

Coaches, instructors and trainers; independent gyms, studios and clubs; creators and educators; and multi-site organisations. Each has a product built for it.

Which product should I choose?

CORE is for solo professionals, GALAXY for single-site venues, PULSAR for creators and educators, and ENTERPRISE for multi-site operators. Not sure? Book a demo and we will help you choose.

Can I offer a customer app with my own branding?

Yes. A branded customer app is available with CORE, GALAXY and as a custom app build.

What happens to my customer data when I switch?

We help you prepare your data for the move. During onboarding we review your current payment setup, explain what can transfer and identify any steps you or your customers may need to take.

Compliance

Compliance without theatre.

fibodo takes compliance seriously — not as a badge, but as an operational responsibility. We align with relevant regulatory frameworks and adapt as requirements evolve.

GDPR & UK GDPR

fibodo supports compliance with EU GDPR and UK GDPR, including lawful processing, data minimisation, security safeguards, data subject rights support and Data Processing Agreements (DPA).

Data Processing Agreements

We provide a standard Data Processing Addendum for customers who require it, covering roles and responsibilities, security measures, sub-processor management and incident notification obligations.

Payments & Financial Data

Payment data is handled by certified payment providers. fibodo does not store raw card details. Responsibility boundaries are clearly defined.

Public Sector & Enterprise Readiness

fibodo supports organisations with due-diligence documentation, security and privacy questionnaires, procurement review processes and risk assessments.

Certifications & Roadmap

Where formal certifications are required, we take a pragmatic approach: aligning internal controls with recognised standards; prioritising real security over checkbox compliance; maintaining a clear compliance roadmap.
